Questions

Who is this for?

Anyone who wants to lose weight, gain weight or muscle, or recomp, and is willing to log honestly. You don't need to be experienced.

Do I need a gym?

Your training is built around what you have access to. Tell me your equipment on the form.

What if I'm not sure it's for me?

You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of your start date. See the Terms.

How do I pay?

After our call I'll send secure payment details (card, or Zelle). You pay once, at signup.

Is my data private?

Only you and I can see it. Photos are private and nothing of yours is ever posted without your written permission. See the Privacy Policy.

Why only 3 spots?

So every client gets real attention, especially in the first month. A new group starts every month.
