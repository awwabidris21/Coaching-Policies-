Awwab Fitness — Nutrition

Recomp

Recomp means building muscle and losing fat at the same time — your calorie target stays flat at maintenance the whole 12 weeks. The real driver here is protein intake and training consistency, not a calorie deficit or surplus.

Your calorie number

Unlike weight loss or gain, your calorie target doesn't step up or down — it holds at your maintenance estimate for the full 12 weeks. Recomp works through consistent resistance training and adequate protein, not calorie manipulation.

Your macro formula

This is the same formula used across weight loss, weight gain, and recomp — only your total calorie number changes between goals, not the macro split:

  • Protein: 0.8g per lb of bodyweight — fixed for the full 12 weeks
  • Fat: 0.3g per lb of bodyweight — fixed for the full 12 weeks
  • Carbs: fill whatever calories remain after protein and fat

Since your total calories hold flat, your macros hold flat too — the real driver of recomp is training to failure and staying consistent, not adjusting food week to week. Your exact numbers are in your calorie calculator.

Sourcing your food based on your situation

The macro targets above are the same for everyone. Which foods you use to hit them depends on your intake answer about grocery budget — ask your coach if you're not sure which applies to you.

Full flexibility

Comfortable grocery budget

  • Protein: chicken, lean beef, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt
  • Carbs: rice, oats, potatoes, fruit
  • Basic macro tracking helps you dial in protein consistency
Some budget flexibility

Working with a moderate budget

  • Budget-friendly protein: eggs, canned tuna, chicken thighs, beans, lentils, cottage cheese
  • Budget-friendly carbs: rice, oats, potatoes
  • Protein first at each meal, then fill the rest with carbs
Limited budget

Keeping it simple and realistic

  • Reliable cheap protein: eggs, canned fish, beans, lentils
  • Reliable cheap carbs: rice, oats, potatoes
  • Training consistency and hitting protein matter more than exact precision here — show up and lift
Track your nutrition (1–5) and exercise intensity in your daily check-in. For recomp, watch exercise intensity closely — the training is doing most of the work here, so consistent effort matters as much as what's on the plate.
