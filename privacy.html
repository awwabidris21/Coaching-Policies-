Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

Awwab's Fitness Coaching ("we", "us") provides personal fitness coaching services, including daily SMS check-in messages to clients who have verbally agreed to receive them.

Information We Collect

We collect your phone number and the responses you provide to our daily check-in text messages (such as energy level, soreness, workout completion, and sleep quality) in order to track your coaching progress.

How We Use Your Information

Your phone number is used solely to send you coaching-related SMS check-ins. Your responses are used only to inform your personal coaching program.

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not share, sell, or rent your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your information is used exclusively for your fitness coaching.

Message Frequency

You will receive approximately one (1) SMS message per day.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting Out

You may opt out of receiving messages at any time by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for assistance.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact your coach directly.
