Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026. Last updated: October 19, 2026.

Awwab Fitness ("we", "us", "your coach") provides personal fitness coaching, the Awwab Fitness app and text message check-ins. This policy explains what we collect, why, who handles it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

1. What we collect

TypeExamples
Account and contactName, email address, phone number, password (stored securely, never visible to us).
Intake informationGoals, schedule, equipment, grocery budget, injuries or medical conditions you choose to share, past coaching, motivation style.
Fitness and health data you logWeight, steps, water, sleep hours, foods and meals, workouts (exercises, weights, reps), daily check-in ratings (nutrition, intensity, sleep, hunger, energy), notes.
Progress photosFront, side and back photos you upload.
MessagesMessages you send your coach in the app and text message replies.
TechnicalYour login session is saved on your device so you stay logged in. We do not run advertising trackers or sell data.

Health and fitness information is sensitive. You choose what to share, and we only ask for what we need to coach you.

2. How we use it

  • To build and adjust your training and nutrition plan, and to hold you accountable.
  • To show your coach your progress, flags and trends, so they can reach out when it matters.
  • To send your text message reminders and check-ins, and to send account emails such as password resets.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3. Who can see your data

  • You and your coach. Other clients cannot see your data. The app is built so each client can only read their own records.
  • Service providers that run the service for us (below). They may only use data to provide their service to us.
  • If required by law or to protect someone's safety.

We will never post your name, photos, weight or results publicly without your separate written permission, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Text messages (SMS)

Message frequency. Up to two (2) messages per day: a morning reminder to log your weigh-in and progress photos, and an evening check-in to log your steps and complete your daily check-in.

No sharing of mobile information. We do not share, sell or rent your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your information is used exclusively for your fitness coaching.

Message and data rates. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting out. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your data while you're a client and for up to 12 months after your program ends, so you can come back without starting over. You can ask us to delete it sooner (section 8). Backups may take a little longer to clear.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Your records and photos are stored in private, access-controlled storage, and each client can only reach their own. Even so, no system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

8. Your choices

  • Access and copy: you can download your own data as a spreadsheet in the app (Notes, then Account, then Download my data). You can also ask us for a copy.
  • Correct or delete: you can ask us to correct or delete your data, including photos, by emailing us. We'll confirm when it's done.
  • Texts: reply STOP at any time.
  • Photos and publicity: you control whether anything of yours is ever shared publicly.

9. Age

The service is for adults (18 and over). We don't knowingly collect data from children.

10. Demo mode

The public demo of the app uses sample data only. Nothing you tap in the demo is collected or saved.

11. This is not a medical service

We are a fitness coaching business, not a healthcare provider, so health privacy laws that apply to doctors and hospitals (such as HIPAA) do not apply to us. We still handle your health and fitness data carefully, as described here.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. If we make a material change we'll let you know in the app or by message.

13. Contact
