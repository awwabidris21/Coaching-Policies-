Your daily routine

WhenWhat
MorningWeigh in before you eat (Home tab). Log how many hours you slept. Take your progress photos when your coach asks (front, side, back).
All dayLog your food (Food tab) or tick your meals (Meals tab). Add water. Search any food, scan a barcode, or tap "Repeat yesterday's foods".
TrainingOpen Training, log the weight and reps for each set. Last session's numbers are shown so you know what to beat. The rest timer starts after each set.
NightEnter your steps and do your daily check-in (nutrition, intensity, sleep). It takes ten seconds.
The one rule: log honestly, even on a bad day. A rough day logged is far more useful than a blank one. Your coach isn't judging, they're using the data to help you.

Your text messages

You'll get two texts a day: a morning reminder to weigh in, log your sleep and take your photos, and an evening reminder for your steps and check-in. You can reply to the evening text with your scores if you're away from the app. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Reaching your coach

Use the Notes tab to message your coach any time: hunger, soreness, travel, questions, schedule changes. Your coach sees it and replies in the app. You'll also get check-in calls.

Common questions

I missed a day. Did I break everything?

No. Log today and keep going. Streaks are motivation, not a punishment.

I ate something that's not in my plan.

Log it. Search it, scan the barcode, or tap "Enter calories and macros yourself". Honest data beats a perfect day.

I trained a different day than scheduled.

On the Training tab, pick the session you actually did. Your plan adjusts around you.

I can't log in or forgot my password.

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen for a reset email, or message your coach.

Who can see my data and photos?

Only you and your coach. Nothing is posted anywhere without your separate written permission. See the Privacy Policy and Terms.
